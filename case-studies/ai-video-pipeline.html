Internal AI video pipeline

Workflow toolingClaude API, SeedanceD5XR, internal

DraftPrototyping with AI video meant sending client material to third-party tools, paying per experiment, and relying on whoever knew how to prompt well. I built an internal pipeline that cuts prototyping costs and generates video prompts through the Claude API, so the know-how lives in the system rather than in one person's head.

Draft: facts to work in

  • Prototyping at 420p and 720p costs around two thirds less than it did with off-the-shelf tools.
  • Outputs land straight in Box rather than sitting indefinitely on a consumer video tool, which matters for confidential client material.
  • It is not fully in-house: generation goes through an API that connects to Seedance. Worth saying plainly so the page doesn't overclaim.
